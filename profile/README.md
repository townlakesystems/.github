Town Lake Systems Group designs and repairs the CRM and go-to-market systems that mid-market B2B technology companies run on: how leads are matched and routed, how accounts and sites are structured, how pipeline is attributed, and where AI agents can take routine work off a team. Michael W. Green founded the firm in 2026.

This GitHub organization holds the firm's public work: reference implementations, sample deliverables, and tools. Client work is never published here.

## What we do

| Service | What it covers |
|---|---|
| CRM and GTM architecture | Objects, fields, lifecycle stages, and routing rules built around how the company actually sells |
| Data quality and attribution | Duplicate and hierarchy cleanup, a written attribution model, and pipeline and funnel reporting |
| AI agents and automation | Agents and n8n workflows that triage, route, and summarize work inside the CRM, with a person approving anything that changes a record or reaches a customer |

## How an engagement runs

1. **Discover:** map the current systems, data, and handoffs, and talk to the people who use them.
2. **Design:** agree the target data model, routing rules, and reports in writing before anything is built.
3. **Build:** configure in a sandbox, test against real scenarios, and migrate in small steps.
4. **Hand off:** train admins and users, and leave documentation the team can maintain on its own.
